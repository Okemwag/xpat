# Synthetic demonstration snapshots

These are generated from the four invented properties in `tests/fixtures/exposure.csv` and the artificial training labels in `tests/fixtures/training.csv`. They are not the supplied 600-property Nairobi portfolio, not real observations, and not evidence of predictive performance in Nairobi.

demo-report.json: baseline result.
demo-model.json: coefficients trained solely on the artificial training fixture.
enhanced-demo.json: same portfolio/curves/frequencies with demo ML uplift applied. Illustrates propagation into monetary loss, not accuracy improvement.
